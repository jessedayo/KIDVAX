About KIDVAX

Keeping children healthy, one vaccine at a time.

🌍 What is KIDVAX?

KIDVAX is a web-based Child Vaccination Tracking System designed to help parents and guardians manage their children's vaccination records digitally. We believe no child should miss a vaccine simply because of a lost paper card or a forgotten schedule.

❓ Why KIDVAX?

In many communities, vaccination records are kept on paper cards that can easily get lost, damaged, or forgotten. KIDVAX solves this by:

  • Storing all vaccination records digitally and securely
  • Automatically tracking upcoming and overdue vaccines
  • Sending email reminders before vaccines are due
  • Providing a clear visual status for every vaccine
